What is actually being modeled?

LCAM: For each rider–driver pair, score pickup distance, predicted cancellation risk, wait age and immediate trip economics, then solve a one-to-one minimum-cost bipartite assignment (with the option to leave requests unmatched).

DVAM: Use the same assignment solver, but subtract λ × estimated future driver-location value from each edge cost. Zone-level estimates update through a simplified temporal-difference rule as demand and supply change. This is a Bellman-inspired teaching approximation, not a trained DQN or a faithful implementation of Uber's proprietary matching policy.

Both modes receive identical seeded arrival streams; decisions change driver locations and availability, generating feedback. Adjust λ or the seed to examine when future-aware dispatch helps—and when it does not. All numbers are synthetic and should not be interpreted as observed Uber performance.
